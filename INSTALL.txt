Replace these three files in GitHub, preserving their paths:
  scripts/build.py
  assets/site.css
  .pages.yml

Pages CMS > Exhibitions and events:
- Add an event, with its date, optional end date, location, link, image, and description.
- Published: enabled for a live entry.
- Feature prominently: enabled for the large featured area; otherwise it appears in the running year list (2027 onward).
- Upcoming / Past event is determined automatically by event end date.

The existing 2025 and 2026 history remains editable under Site pages > EXHIBITIONS > Page content. The archive shows a thin pink line above each year and smaller year headings.
