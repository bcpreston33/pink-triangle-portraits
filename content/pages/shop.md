### Supporting the Project

At its heart, Pink Triangle Portraits is about education, remembrance, and preserving the stories of LGBTQ+ people whose lives and experiences were hidden, erased, or nearly lost to history. Through art and research, the project seeks to bring these individuals and their stories back into the light, ensuring they are remembered and shared with future generations.

***Selling these pieces or prints is not the purpose of this project.*** It is simply one way for those who wish to support the work to help keep it going. Proceeds from purchases help cover art supplies, research materials, and the cost of locating and obtaining rare books, films, historical documents, and other resources that are often difficult to find or access.

Every purchase helps make further research and new portraits possible, but supporting the project can be as simple as exploring the stories, sharing them with others, and helping keep this history alive.
