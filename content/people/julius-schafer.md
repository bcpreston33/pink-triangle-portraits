Thanks to the extraordinary research of Davide Napolitano, I have a story for someone who had none before.

Julius Matthias “Julla” Schäfer was born on October 22, 1908 in Düsseldorf, Germany. Julius sang, played the guitar, and wrote songs. He was a very intelligent person. In Düsseldorf prison, he came up with a way to communicate with the adjacent cells by using the hollow metal tubes of the beds. In the concentration camp, he managed to make a pencil and to find paper for the group leader Robert Ölbermann. He was a free spirited young man and loved traveling. He never denied his queerness under interrogation. Julius openly opposed Nazi ideology, and he took part in a strike by left wing workers in Düsseldorf.

He attended school until the age of 16 and did not perform compulsory military service. Julius was a member of the Nerotherbund, a scouting organization similar to the Boy Scouts, where he was the leader of the Order of the Pirates and head of the order in the Lower Rhine district.

After 1933, the youth movement was made illegal. Julius changed jobs several times and traveled abroad, going to Turkey, Africa, Spain, Greece, and Iceland. These trips were organized by his youth association.

On Feb 13, 1936, the leaders of the youth movement were arrested. In July, Julius was sentenced to 7 months in prison. After release, he was arrested again because the Gestapo believed that Julius would still continue to have sex with men. This time, Julius was not kept in prison but was transferred first to the Lichtenburg concentration camp, and then to Sachsenhausen.

The concentration camp experience was terrible. Julius’ friends later said that the camp broke and changed him, and he never smiled anymore. In Oct. 1941, Julius was accused of belonging to an illegal German youth group, but the charges were dropped. In 1942, he became engaged to a woman, and in 1944 became the father of a child.

He disappeared in June in Belarus while assigned to the army. I created this new portrait of Julla in honor of Davide's work.
