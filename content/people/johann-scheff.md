Johann Scheff was born on August 28, 1915, one of 11 siblings. He was arrested by the Nazis at just 16 years old. In the face of unimaginable terror, he smirked back at his captors, a quiet act of defiance against a regime that sought to erase him and others like him. 

Johann was known to wear women's clothing sometimes, and did sex work as a young man. He was arrested alongside a man he was living with. He was arrested and sentenced multiple times for being gay. His name appears on prisoner transport lists during WWII. We know he survived because he again shows up in the record because of being arrested again in 1952 for violating Paragraph 175. His fate is unknown after this.
