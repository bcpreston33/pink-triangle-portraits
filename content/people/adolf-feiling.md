Adolf Feiling was born May 29, 1912. He worked as a laborer before his arrest on September 21, 1939. He was sent to Floßenbürg concentration camp, where he was forced to sign his own castration order, a cruel and dehumanizing injustice. He remained imprisoned there until his death on March 29, 1944, at just 31 years old.
